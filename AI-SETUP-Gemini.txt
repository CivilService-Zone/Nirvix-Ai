ENABLE GEMINI AI:
1. Open Google AI Studio: https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey
2. Create/copy a Gemini API key. Keep it private.
3. Open your hosting service's server Environment/Variables settings.
4. Add:
   GEMINI_API_KEY = your_private_gemini_api_key
   AI_PROVIDER = gemini
   GEMINI_MODEL = gemini-2.5-flash
5. Save and redeploy/restart the Node.js server.
6. Open https://YOUR-DOMAIN/api/health and confirm aiConfigured is true and aiProvider is gemini.
7. Test AI Help with a real question. API availability, quota, and billing/rate limits are controlled by Google.

SECURITY:
- Never paste GEMINI_API_KEY into index.html or client-side code.
- Do not include your API key in the ZIP or share it publicly.
- If Gemini fails, the website's AI Help shows:
